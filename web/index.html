Architecture

Bronze → Silver → Gold

The same progressive-data-quality layering used in modern lakehouse platforms, built in SQL. Raw exports land as-is, get cleaned and validated, then surface as analysis-ready facts, dimensions and KPIs.

BRONZE

Raw ingestion

CSVs loaded verbatim, every column as VARCHAR, with a surrogate bronze_id for ordered dedup.

  • Zero transformation or business logic
  • Source fidelity preserved — malformed values captured, not dropped
  • AUTO_INCREMENT key tracks load order
→
SILVER

Cleaned & validated

Type casting, dedup, text standardization and business-rule validation on every row.

  • Text → DATE / DECIMAL / INT casting
  • Totals recomputed & mismatch-flagged
  • Lateness recalculated from shipping dates
  • Orphan foreign-key checks
→
GOLD

Business-ready layer

Star schema of dimensions, facts and pre-aggregated KPI views for reporting.

  • dim_customer · dim_product · dim_supplier
  • fact_sales · fact_procurement
  • Supplier / product / monthly summary KPIs
  • Views, not tables — always in sync with Silver

Headline KPIs

Revenue on the sales side, risk on the supply side

Two fact streams — customer sales and supplier procurement — with KPIs derived from the Silver-layer validated fields, exactly as the Power BI measures compute them.

Sales Analytics

20,000 customer orders, one clean view

Monthly trends, product profitability, customer mix and logistics performance — all built on the recalculated (not blindly trusted) order fields.

Procurement Analytics

2,000 purchase orders: spend & delivery risk

Supplier spend concentration, regional exposure and — most importantly — the gap between what suppliers report about their on-time delivery and what the data actually shows.

Supplier Performance

Reported vs. actual on-time delivery

Each supplier carries a self-reported on-time rate. The pipeline recalculates the real rate from planned vs. actual delivery dates. Sort, search, and see who over-promises.

Data Quality

Validation isn't a step — it's the pipeline

Every Silver-layer load runs automated checks: totals are recomputed from raw fields, lateness is recalculated from dates, orphan keys are surfaced, and row counts are reconciled with Bronze.
